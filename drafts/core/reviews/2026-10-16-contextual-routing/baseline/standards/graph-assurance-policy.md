# Graph assurance policy: tensor-graph-assurance/3

Semantic review revision: 2026-10-13 (`tensor-graph-assurance/3`, routing profile 2 and semantic profile 1). Previous routing policy 2 remains historical. Original decision date: 2026-10-11. Applies prospectively to generated reports and authoring tools for the dated investigation graph. It applies to repaired working graph 0.20261013b and changes no graph schema, published artifact, investigation-record Core contract or historical report. Passing these checks is author-side acceptance of a bounded graph/evidence package; publication, independent review and institutional acceptance are separate decisions.

## Blocking acceptance

Default lint and the compatibility spelling `--draft-review` apply the same policy. There are no score-based exceptions or lowered thresholds.

1. **Graph authoring:** IDs, references, labels, archetypes, complete yes/no/unknown outcome coverage, unique route identities, existing endpoints, reachable nodes and terminal structure. Multiple targets, independently bound cross-domain leads and fresh-instance revisits are supported; ID ordering, DAG topology and fan-in counts are diagnostic properties rather than evidence prerequisites.
2. **Structural properties:** all nodes must be reachable and every nonterminal outcome covered, and all seven domains by eight archetypes present. Domain/archetype presence is a scope check, not proof of substantive investigative coverage.
3. **Evidence cards:** every active question must have a matching public predicate, role, domain, bound fields, assessment record requirements, version annotation and explicit yes/no/unknown criteria. The seven-path base role bindings and any explicitly versioned additive evidence profile must cover every active question exactly once. Evidence criteria must pin the exact graph digest, graph version and schema identity.
4. **Evidence transitions:** every exact node, card and edge is bound to the separately reviewed snapshot. Outcomes may offer several explicitly selected leads; no answer supplies a target finding. Refuted or unknown hypotheses retain their answer while independently evidenced scope, relationships, source and effects may continue. Each resumed assessment rechecks its own accepted observation, complete identity, time bounds, discriminator and method. Independent observations compare lineage against every primary record. Cross-domain targets declare their own exact question, graph/profile/version, full subjects, interval and cited discovery basis. Source question applicability and actual answer/context remain explicit. A partial unknown source may be handed off with precisely enumerated missing context, never invented bounds. Readiness checks are a draft authoring profile; Core outcome guards do not execute them automatically.
5. **Scenario agreement:** the nonempty supplied suite must agree with explicit expected paths and states under the normalized evaluator. Exact graph, criteria, suite, policy and implementation digests accompany reports. No arbitrary expressions, evidence fetching or tool execution is performed.
6. **Scenario coverage:** every entry and classification node needs yes/no/unknown cases. Scope, correlation, attribution and impact need all three assessments in each domain. Each domain retains a public-report-only case that leaves missing victim evidence unresolved. This is a bounded minimum test profile, not exhaustive path or real-world coverage.
7. **Fail closed:** absent/malformed evidence inputs, ambiguous scenario inheritance, mismatched pins, missing checks, or changes to the reference-gate catalog cannot yield a passing acceptance result. Full acceptance requires a generated graph artifact; JSONL-only lint emits diagnostics but remains incomplete until the exact graph and evidence package are assessed.

The canonical draft automatically selects its adjacent criteria and scenario suite. Other graph artifacts require explicit `--criteria-path` and `--scenarios-path`. A historical artifact lacking those new inputs can be inspected but cannot claim current acceptance. Published historical reports retain their original policy and meanings.

## Current semantic profile: tensor-semantic-review/1

The [item-level review](../drafts/core/reviews/2026-10-13-semantic/README.md) records separate AI domain and routing reviewers, their baseline defects, independently authored challenges, repaired-snapshot verdicts and exact pins. These checks do not constitute institutional approval or authenticated incident evidence.

- Every record is evaluated with its whole finite, nonnegative clock uncertainty bound in a half-open interval. Missing clocks and partial identities remain unknown; list order cannot select a witness.
- A hypothesis is mandatory for testing that hypothesis. Independent scope, relationship, technical-source, behavior-pattern and metric questions use their own evidence, retaining any supplied hypothesis meaning and actual answer. Refutation is not an assertion of a benign event.
- Scope uses complete declared entity identities. A documented dependency names the relation ID and direction, not just matching endpoints. Relationships preserve separate source/target intervals. Metrics preserve comparable units and a separately bounded baseline, which may precede the observed event.
- Profile draft.2 separates rule/deletion attempts by operation identity, declares fact types and metadata/content dependencies, and uses explicitly declared corroboration discriminators and namespaces or mappings. Observation-only state predicates do not claim historical change or an action.
- A generic terminal retains the actual incoming focus, completed answers and explicitly open questions. Each fixed-facet row retains its real facet; every assessed focus needs an applicable nonterminal assessment. Exact supplied hypothesis meaning and actual trace answers/foci survive. A terminal ends only that route, never the investigation.
- Public predicates observe technical facts. Vendor tools, queries and adapters implement acquisition above the graph; company policy, approvals, business significance and response authority belong in separate layers.

Exact structure/criteria pins reject unreviewed edits; independently authored counterexamples test behavior beyond the builder's fixtures. Neither is a proof of every possible path or universal investigative completeness. The profile is a draft interpretation dependency within existing extension fields, not a new Core binding or schema.

## Historical reference thresholds

Retain every original formula, value and threshold under `monitoring.legacyReferenceThresholds`. Current `monitoring.publishGates` describes the blocking checks above, with policy identity and a clear author-side scope. No historical object is rewritten or relabeled in place. The release-contract validator continues to validate the exact published snapshots under their existing contract.

| Historical threshold | Decision and reason |
| DAG / increasing question IDs / one destination per answer | Historical authoring shape, not an investigation invariant. The working draft permits multiple targets, independently bound domain pivots and fresh-instance revisits. Evidence prerequisites and explicit selections remain required. |
|---|---|
| Cross-domain edges >= 25% | Diagnostic only. A label-change edge count does not prove an evidence relationship. A single-domain hypothesis can be sound, and cross-domain observations can be explicitly related without changing the source route context. Check context and target bindings instead. |
| Routing score >= 100 | Diagnostic only. It combines existing structural checks with a 20-point cross-domain-quota term. Keep the structural checks directly; do not count the quota twice. |
| Robustness score >= 85 | Diagnostic only. It is 100 times targeted node-removal reachability, duplicating that threshold. Deleting prerequisite questions is not missing-evidence handling. |
| Targeted reachability >= 0.85 after deleting 5% of questions | Maintenance sensitivity diagnostic. Removing questions from a versioned definition is a content change, not an operational outage. Bypassing a necessary validator to keep paths reachable would be unsafe. Missing evidence must use explicit unknown routes. |
| Betweenness centralization <= 0.02 | Maintenance concentration diagnostic. The implementation measures maximum node betweenness divided by summed betweenness, not normalized Freeman centralization. The value is sensitive to graph replication and granularity; required evidence checks can legitimately be hubs. |
| Overall score >= 80 | Diagnostic only. Uncalibrated weighted aggregation repeats the quota and deletion simulation and includes terminal-domain entropy. A scalar must not compensate for a failed safety property or block sound evidence semantics. |
| Nonnegative information gain | Diagnostic only, although currently passing. This is entropy over terminal-domain labels under assumed uniform branches. It measures neither investigation truth nor calibrated uncertainty, and entropy concavity makes nonnegative gain expected by construction. It must not be presented as a semantic safeguard. |

Thresholds and weights were not calibrated against investigator outcomes in the reviewed implementation or authoring documentation. Restoring a numeric blocker requires a defined threat/failure model, relevant measured outcomes, representative independent evidence, calibrated thresholds, demonstrated resistance to padding/renaming/bypass incentives, and a recorded policy decision. Current diagnostic recommendations must not tell authors to bypass evidence prerequisites.

## Limits and release handling

Evidence cards and synthetic expected paths can be authored consistently while still being wrong. The evaluator trusts supplied provenance and fact assessments and supports only its documented discriminators. Mechanical acceptance does not establish source honesty, hypothesis correctness, policy compliance, causal effects, independent interoperability or institutional review. Do not call `allPassed` publication authorization.

The builder checks structure and creates only a working draft or explicitly requested new unpromoted graph directory. Preparation alone cannot satisfy the evidence gates, update a manifest or move stable pointers. Before any future release, assess the exact prepared artifact with its matching evidence package and current policy, then complete the recorded release checklist. Schema and historical artifacts remain immutable.
