# First 90 days of adoption work

This is a proposed work plan, not a calendar commitment or evidence of contact with an institution. Confirm maintainer capacity and consenting owners before scheduling it. The purpose is recognition earned through usable public artifacts and independent evidence.

The [prioritized next-stage plan](next-stage-plan.md) records current readiness, immediate operational closeout and the evidence required before partnership or stable-standard claims.

## Days 1–30: make the contract reviewable

Publish the candidate specification, examples, validator, CLI, browser lab, citable questions, and explicit limitations. Keep the existing graph archive accessible. Triage public issues by semantic ambiguity, interoperability failure, domain content, and usability. Select and record a convenor, editor, conformance maintainer, and private reporting contact through accepted appointments.

Prepare a short invitation using the [reviewer packet](reviewer-packet.md), tailored to a specific contribution. Potential participation categories are university digital-forensics/AI-security researchers, incident-response practitioners, CASE/UCO implementers, public-interest security organizations, and tool vendors. These categories are research targets, not named partners or outreach authorization.

Deliverables: exact candidate artifact set; public issue/decision log; accepted stewardship roster; scoped baseline validation; one written CASE/UCO comparison plan.

## Days 31–60: test independent use

Recruit two independently developed implementations and reviewers with disclosed affiliations. Ask them to exchange the two reference cases, then add one authorized or synthetic scenario from their own practice. Record friction, discarded fields, unsupported dependencies, uncertainty handling, and time needed to understand or reproduce the case. Do not hide failed exchanges.

Test a human-led conventional investigation alongside an agent-related investigation. Keep the first useful task narrow: handing an investigation to another person or tool without losing what was asked, why a conclusion was reached, and what remains unresolved.

Deliverables: bidirectional exchange reports with hashes and versions; two substantive independent reviews; prioritized semantic corrections; one concrete CASE/UCO mapping and loss report.

## Days 61–90: publish evidence and select the next candidate

Resolve findings in a new version where meaning changes. Publish a technical report or case study with consenting coauthors and reproducible synthetic data. Prepare a conference/workshop or standards-community submission around observed results; obtain explicit authorization before submitting or using another organization's name. Open the required review window before a stable-release decision.

Deliverables: revised candidate; public objection/response register; reproducible technical report draft; documented stable-release decision or explicit reasons to remain a candidate.

## Measures

| Measure | How to verify | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent implementation pairs | Authorship/dependency disclosure and bidirectional exchange report | Tests portability beyond one codebase |
| Substantive external reviews | Public review with version, findings, and affiliation | Tests meaning and credibility |
| Published references | Stable URL/DOI and exact reference to TENSOR | Establishes attributable recognition |
| Partnerships | Consented written scope and agreed public wording | Establishes a real relationship |
| Successful handoffs | Case, required meaning, import/export evidence, declared loss | Tests practical utility |
| Review responsiveness | Issue dates, dispositions, and unresolved objections | Tests sustainable stewardship |

Targets for the first review cycle are two independent implementations, two substantive independent reviews, and one reproducible technical report draft. These are proposed targets, not completed outcomes. Website traffic, stars, sponsors, and generated examples can inform discovery; they do not substitute for these adoption measures.
