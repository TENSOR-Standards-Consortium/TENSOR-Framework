## Contact, findings and escalation

- Public, nonsensitive technical findings: use the Framework [Implementation report](https://github.com/TENSOR-Standards-Consortium/TENSOR-Framework/issues/new?template=implementation-report.yml) template. Cite the review ID and pinned artifact.
- Process proposals, disagreement or escalation: use a [Core RFC](https://github.com/TENSOR-Standards-Consortium/TENSOR-Framework/issues/new?template=core-rfc.yml), referencing the original finding. The convenor is accountable for recording a response; no response-time guarantee is offered.
- Vulnerabilities or sensitive reports: use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/TENSOR-Standards-Consortium/TENSOR-Framework/security/advisories/new). See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Reports reach repository administrators; there is no independent or staffed response service claimed.
- A dispute involving the sole steward cannot receive an independent internal appeal today. Keep the objection open and visible, and seek a consenting external reviewer before claiming independent resolution.

The [review process](review-process.md) records finding handling and preserves dissent. No dedicated mailbox is claimed or invented.
