# Governance status

TENSOR's agreed direction is a public standard for all cyber investigations, usable by humans and agents, with vendor and business layers above a shared investigative contract. Implementation and participation must remain independent of purchasing a product or sponsorship.

TENSOR's long-term identity, domains, infrastructure, and stewardship must be independent of any founder's employer, consultancy, or commercial product. Temporary operational support confers no ownership, endorsement, or lasting affiliation. Production infrastructure must be controlled through dedicated TENSOR accounts and documented access responsibilities before an inherited hosting arrangement becomes a permanent dependency.

**The governance process is proposed, not yet adopted.** The name of the GitHub organization is not evidence of incorporation, nonprofit status, an appointed council, or institutional representation. GitHub repository permissions identify technical access, not independent standards authority.

Read the [full governance proposal](proposals/founding-core-v0.1.0/02-governance.md) and [founding charter](proposals/founding-core-v0.1.0/00-charter.md). They propose public decisions, disclosed affiliations and conflicts, review windows, independent review, appeals, and a later representative council. They do not appoint people or create contractual duties.

## Adoption record required

A governance adoption proposal should identify the text revision, named consenting role holders, affiliations, conflicts, review dates, objections and their dispositions, appeal route, and the decision. It must identify the applicable content, code, contribution, trademark, and patent policies without assuming that an existing license supplies new permissions or obligations.

The [existing Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) is preserved. Any proposed changes to rights or stewardship need review by the relevant owners. No sponsor receives privileged semantics, conformance status, suppression of negative results, or extra decision power through this package.

Use a [Core RFC](https://github.com/TENSOR-Standards-Consortium/TENSOR-Framework/issues/new?template=core-rfc.yml) to propose a governance change. Public participation does not require a membership purchase. Do not post sensitive reports there; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
